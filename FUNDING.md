# Funding

> **Status: proposed**, with [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md). Changing these rules takes two thirds of all Core
> members.

Nobody is paid from Sunrise's money. What comes in pays for what keeps Sunrise running, in the open.

## Today

Sunrise accepts no money yet. Its running costs (hosting, CI, domains) are paid by Core members personally, and
they are not paid back.

## Where money goes in

- **One public pot.** Every donation, sponsorship and any other income goes to Sunrise's account at a fiscal host
  the Core Team picks (Open Collective Europe is the first candidate), never to anyone's personal account. Fiscal
  hosts take a fee from what comes in.
- When Sunrise's non-profit association exists, the pot moves to it.
- Income from any deal Sunrise makes goes into the pot like everything else.
- Sunrise's money comes only from donations and sponsorships, never from selling anything to players.

## What it pays for, in order

1. **Infrastructure:** CI runners, hosting, the site, storage and bandwidth.
2. **Services the project needs:** domains, code signing certificates, and other accounts and services named in
   the budget.
3. **Reserve:** three months of 1 and 2 kept in the pot.
4. **Anything else**, only by a Core vote, once 1 to 3 are covered.

The Core Team votes a **budget** once (for example hosting, CI and domains up to stated amounts). Payments inside it
need no new vote, but each is approved on the fiscal host by two Core members, neither of them the person being
paid. Anything outside the budget is a Core vote ([GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md)).

Tools one person uses (an AI subscription, a personal machine) are not paid from the pot unless 1 to 3 are covered
and a Core vote approves it, with the amount and the reason public.

## What nobody gets

- **No salaries, fees, profit shares or dividends** for anyone, Core members included.
- **Reimbursement only:** a documented cost someone paid for the project can be paid back from the budget, with the
  receipt public. The person being paid back doesn't approve it.
- **No paid features, no shop and no advertisements**, ever, in the launcher, the game or anything else Sunrise ships.
  This is one of the rules that are never voted on ([GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md)).

## In the open

- Every income and expense is visible on the fiscal host's public page.
- The Core Team posts a short summary each quarter: what came in, what went out, and what the pot holds.
- Every money vote is recorded with each member's vote.

## If Sunrise stops

Whatever is left goes, by Core vote, to a non-profit or an open-source project, never to a member.
