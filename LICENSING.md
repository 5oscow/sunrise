# Licensing FAQ

Sunrise's code is under the [PolyForm Shield licence](LICENSE). This page explains it in plain words; it isn't
part of the licence, and where they differ, the licence decides.

## What's fine without asking

- **Playing**, and everything a player does with Sunrise.
- **Running servers**, including selling ranks, perks, cosmetics or anything else on your server.
- **Hosting**: renting out Sunrise servers or offering them in a hosting panel.
- **Mods**, resource packs, data packs and shader packs for Sunrise, under any licence. Selling them is a matter
  for Mojang's EULA and usage guidelines, which Sunrise's licence neither adds to nor overrides.
- **Videos, streams and guides** about Sunrise, monetized or not.
- **Changing Sunrise** for your own use or your server, and sharing those changes. Sharing a whole fork is covered
  below.

## Free forks and ports

Forks and ports are fine without asking, including ones for other platforms or devices (a mobile or handheld port,
a fork tuned for older hardware), as long as they stay free: nothing sold, no ads, no paid features, and no money
for any person, though donations into a fully public fund are fine. Keep the same licence and publish the source,
credit Sunrise without calling yourself Sunrise, and don't put it into another Minecraft client or launcher. The
[additional permission at the top of LICENSE](LICENSE) has the exact conditions.

## What isn't allowed

Forking or repackaging Sunrise's code into a product that competes with Sunrise and makes money: a paid or
ad-supported client, launcher or repack, or putting Sunrise's code into another Minecraft client or launcher. This
holds whatever licence that product uses.

## What else to know

- **Code that came from Horizon** is also available under its original MIT licence
  ([LICENSES/MIT-Horizon.txt](LICENSES/MIT-Horizon.txt)). The licence above only restricts Sunrise's own code.
- **Contributing:** there is no CLA. Contributors keep their copyright and their code comes in under the same
  licence it goes out under; commits are signed off under the Developer Certificate of Origin
  ([CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md#licence-and-dco)). Sunrise never relicenses contributors' code under paid,
  proprietary or any other terms.
- **When you share Sunrise**, pass on the licence (or its URL) and the `Required Notice:` line at the top of
  [LICENSE](LICENSE).
